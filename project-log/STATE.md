## What changed against the plan

with the evidence.

| Set at intake | Measured | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Dedup on locality + area + beds + baths | Too coarse — 77.1% of rows in multi-groups. Rebuilt on coordinates + locality path: 33.0% | D-43 |
| Collapse groups within 15% price spread | No threshold has anything to separate — high-spread groups are not false merges | D-45 |
| Stop when flagged groups fall below 5% of rows | Unreachable without putting price in the fingerprint. 2-day cap governs | D-38 |
| Title beats payload on area | True for the unit, false for the quantity — reversed mid-stage | D-32 |
| B-13's seven zero-variance columns | Fourteen are single-valued; two B-13 listed are not | D-37 |
